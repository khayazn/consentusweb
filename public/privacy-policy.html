🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Consentus ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, age, gender, and profile photos.
  • Location Data: Your approximate location to show nearby matches (only when you grant permission).
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, including matches, messages, and preferences.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • Verification Documents: If you choose to verify your identity, we collect government-issued ID, proof of residence, and company registration documents (if applicable).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Connect you with compatible people based on your preferences and location.
  • Facilitate communication between you and your matches.
  • Improve our app features and user experience.
  • Send you notifications about matches, messages, and app updates.
  • Ensure the safety and security of our platform.
  • Verify your identity and display a 🟢 Verified badge on your profile.

3. Identity Verification

Consentus offers a voluntary identity verification feature to help build trust within our community. When you choose to verify your identity:

  • We collect and securely store verification documents including government-issued ID, proof of residence, and company registration (if applicable).
  • Your verification documents are reviewed by our admin team to confirm your identity.
  • Once verified, your account receives a 🟢 Verified badge visible to other users.
  • Your documents are stored securely and are never shared with other users.
  • We retain verification documents for as long as your account is active and you maintain your verified status.
  • If you delete your account or your verification is revoked, your documents will be securely deleted.
  • All verification documents are handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws (GDPR, POPIA).
🔐 Your Verification Data is Secure
All verification documents are encrypted and stored securely. They are only accessed by authorized admin personnel for the sole purpose of verifying your identity.

4. Location Data

Consentus uses your location to show you people nearby. Your location is:

  • Never shared publicly with other users.
  • Only used to calculate approximate distances between users.
  • Stored securely and not used for any other purpose.
  • Accessible only with your explicit permission.

You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect the security and integrity of our platform.
  • With service providers who assist in operating our app (e.g., hosting, payment processing).
  • Verification documents are never shared with third parties or other users.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Strict access controls and authentication protocols.
  • Secure storage of verification documents with limited admin access.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Request deletion of your verification documents if you no longer wish to remain verified.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services. Verification documents are retained while you maintain verified status and are securely deleted upon:

  • Your request to remove verification.
  • Account deletion.
  • Revocation of your verified status.
  • Inactivity for a period determined by our retention policy.

9. Children's Privacy

Consentus is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We take appropriate measures to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
